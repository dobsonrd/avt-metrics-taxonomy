    Tier 1 - Minimum Viable Assurance (Quick Reference)¶

    Legend: tier and cadence icons

    Priority tier (leading dot in each metric heading):

    • 🟢 Tier 1 - Minimum viable assurance (measurable today with existing tools)
    • 🟡 Tier 2 - Recommended for any AVT deployment
    • 🔵 Tier 3 - Advanced / research-grade

    Measurement cadence (used in the Tier 1 quick-reference bullets):

    • 🚪 One-off gate - measured once pre-deployment as an acceptance criterion
    • 📡 Continuous - automated, ongoing measurement during operational use
    • 🔄 Periodic audit - scheduled assessment (quarterly / annual)

    Other flags:

    • ⚠️ Metric carries an underspecification warning - see the full entry for measurement-science caveats

    See How to use for the full definitions.

    The smallest set of metrics that a deployer cannot responsibly skip. All are measurable today with existing tools, data, and governance capacity.

    ⚠️ This list is a calibrated starting point, not a fixed checklist. Local deployment context — specialty mix, patient population, platform maturity, governance capacity, risk appetite, and volume — shifts both tier assignments and threshold numbers. A deployment with elevated risk on any of these axes should promote relevant Tier 2 or Tier 3 metrics to Tier 1; a deployment with low risk on a given axis may treat a Tier 1 metric as Tier 2 with explicit justification. See the Calibration & Context principle for the structural commitment, the six axes, and the local-calibration documentation expectation.

    The current Tier 1 set comprises 58 countable metrics. Tier 1 expanded substantially with the January–March 2026 NHS guidance suite and again in v5.3.0 / v5.4.0 following the FTS notice 069369-2025; entries promoted or minted in those waves are marked (new in v5.3/v5.4) or (promoted v5.3) below. For NHS deployers, the shape of Day Zero minimum assurance has changed materially since early-2025 vendor procurement; re-assess existing deployments against the expanded set.

    Multi-actor responsibilities are counted once per actor, so per-actor totals below sum to more than 58.

    Cadence icons:

    • 🚪 = pre-deployment gate (Prevention layer)
    • 📡 = continuous in-service signal (Detection or Limitation layer)
    • 🔄 = periodic content audit (Detection layer)

    Tier 1 by Responsible Actor¶

    Deployer¶

    TP · Audio Capture & Environment (Technical Pipeline)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    TP.AC-5 Microphone & Hardware Validation 🚪 Basic pre-deployment hardware check. No AVT should go live without confirming capture hardware meets minimum specifications.

    TP · Summarisation / NLP (Technical Pipeline)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    TP.SN-5 Hallucination Rate ⚠️ 🔄 Core safety metric. NAS Day Zero SPI with <2% review and ≥5% pause thresholds. See underspecification warning — reported rates span 1–67% across the literature.
    TP.SN-6 Omission Rate 🔄 Arguably more dangerous than hallucination because omissions are invisible to the reviewer. Must be included in periodic clinical note audit.
    TP.SN-15 Negation Handling Accuracy 🔄 Safety-critical and well-documented LLM failure mode. Negation errors directly cause clinical harm.
    TP.SN-20 Uncertainty Marker Preservation 🔄 Safety-critical: certainty inflation creates false diagnostic confidence in the record.

    TP · Downstream Write-back (Technical Pipeline)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    TP.WB-1 Write-back Fidelity 🚪 Highest-priority pre-deployment gate. A hallucinated allergy written to the EPR allergy field is a system-level safety failure that propagates to every future clinical decision.
    TP.WB-2 Integration Error Rate 📡 Standard integration monitoring. Automated, low-burden, catches data pipeline failures that directly affect patient records.
    TP.WB-3 Field Mapping Accuracy 🚪 Safety-critical pre-deployment test. Wrong-field placement of safety-critical content (allergies, medications) bypasses downstream safety mechanisms entirely.
    TP.WB-4 Update vs Append Behaviour 🚪 Safety-critical pre-deployment test. Overwriting existing safety-critical data is a patient safety event.
    TP.WB-8 PRSB Semantic Completeness (new in v5.3) 📡 Proportion of PRSB-mandatory information elements present in AVT-generated output, stratified by applicable PRSB standard (CIS, Outpatient Letter, Discharge, etc.).

    HL · Human Factors & Workflow (Human Layer)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    HL.HF-1 Edit Rate (% Notes Edited) 📡 Primary continuous complacency indicator. NAS Day Zero SPI. The single most important human factors metric — trajectory reveals automation bias before incidents occur.
    HL.HF-3a Review-Before-Signing Rate 📡 NAS Day Zero SPI with ≥95% threshold and <85% pause trigger. Directly monitors whether human oversight is functioning.
    HL.HF-3b Time-to-Sign Distribution 📡 The tail of very-fast approvals (<5 seconds for complex notes) is the safety-critical population. Distribution analysis detects rubber-stamping patterns.

    IO · Patient Experience (Impact & Outcomes)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    IO.PX-1 Patient Opt-Out Rate 📡 Low-burden continuous monitoring. Rising rates signal trust issues. Demographic disaggregation reveals consent model equity.

    IO · Fairness & Equity (Impact & Outcomes)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    IO.FE-1 Deployment Equity Index (promoted v5.3) 📡 Deployment equity disaggregated by site, setting, and demographic axes. Complements cluster-level disaggregated metrics.

    GV · Safety & Governance (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.SG-9 Safety Performance Indicators with Thresholds (DSCMS) 📡 The governance mechanism that converts metrics into action. Without pre-defined thresholds and escalation paths, metrics are information without teeth.
    GV.SG-11 Adverse Event / Incident Rate (LFPSE) 📡 Established national reporting. The ultimate lagging indicator — by the time this metric moves, harm has occurred.
    GV.SG-13 Assurance Debt Accumulation Rate 📡 The honest governance metric. Every deployer will accumulate it; making it visible prevents governance theatre.
    GV.SG-14 Near-Miss Reporting Rate 📡 Essential leading indicator — the early warning system that LFPSE is the lagging indicator of.
    GV.SG-17 Hazard Log Completeness 📡 Regulatory requirement under DCB0129. Compliance obligation, not a recommendation.

    GV · Operational (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.OP-1 Documentation Time per Consultation 📡 Most widely measured benefit metric. Tells you nothing about safety but essential for demonstrating value proposition.
    GV.OP-6 Adoption Rate & Selective Use Patterns 📡 Selective adoption patterns (avoiding AVT for complex cases) reveal practical system boundaries and are diagnostically valuable.

    GV · Privacy & Data Governance (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.PD-1 Audio Retention Compliance 📡 UK GDPR storage limitation requirement. Non-compliance is a regulatory breach.
    GV.PD-8 Consent Verification Accuracy 📡 CQC Mythbuster 109 requires patients to be informed. Process compliance is measurable today.
    GV.PD-9 Cross-Border Data Transfer Compliance 🚪 Legal compliance requirement. Must be assessed at procurement. Non-compliance is a regulatory breach.
    GV.PD-10 Subject Access Request Fulfilment 🚪 Legal compliance requirement. Must be tested before go-live to confirm vendor supports SAR fulfilment workflow.
    GV.PD-11 Right to Erasure Compliance 🚪 Legal compliance requirement. Must be tested before go-live to understand erasure scope and limitations.
    GV.PD-2 Audio Time-to-Deletion 📡 NHSE IG guidance March 2026 requires deletion after summary sign-off. Must verify deletion in primary storage, caches, and backups.
    GV.PD-3 Transcript Retention Compliance 📡 Higher-risk than audio (structured, searchable, readily consumable). Explicit retention policy required; compliance measurable continuously.
    GV.PD-16 Decommissioning Data Handling Compliance (new in v4.1) 🚪 End-of-life data handling per the deployer-vendor contract. Limitation-layer infrastructure that bounds damage at retirement.
    GV.PD-13 Privacy Notice Currency & Completeness (new in v5.3) 🚪 Each public-facing privacy notice must include an AVT-specific section with lawful basis, controller/processor relationship, retention, and any model-training-use disclosure. NHSE IG Attestation family.
    GV.PD-18 Information Asset Register Completeness (new in v5.4) 🚪 AVT system listed in the IAR with named Information Asset Owner, lawful basis, retention, sub-processor list, and risk classification.

    GV · Training & Competency (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.TC-1 Clinician Training Completion Rate 📡 Governance requirement. No clinician should use AVT without completing required training — binary compliance, 100% target.

    GV · Vendor Transparency & Contractual (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.VT-7 Sub-Processor Transparency 📡 Legal compliance requirement under UK GDPR Article 28. Must be assessed at procurement and monitored for changes.

    GV · NHS Compliance & Regulatory (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.CR-4 AVT Supplier Registry Listing Verification 🚪 Procurement gate with trivial verification cost. Non-listed vendors should not be deployed.
    GV.CR-5 ICB Engagement Documentation 🚪 Pre-deployment compliance gate under CIO/CCIO guidance v2. Absence indicates governance process breakdown.
    GV.CR-6 Clinical Safety Case Completeness 🔄 DCB0129/0160 regulatory requirement. The 2025 FOI finding that many NHS digital health deployments lack compliant safety cases makes active monitoring essential.
    GV.CR-7 DPIA Template Completion Rate 🚪 UK GDPR Article 35 legal requirement. Use of the NHSE March 2026 template enables cross-deployment comparison.
    GV.CR-1 Patient Dissent Recording Rate 📡 Per-encounter compliance with the requirement to document and respect objections. Distinct from aggregate opt-out: dissent recording is procedural integrity at the point of care.
    GV.CR-2 Verbal Notification Compliance 🔄 Proportion of AVT consultations where verbal notification was delivered at session start. The compliance metric behind the consent model.
    GV.CR-3 AI-Generated Content Labelling Compliance 📡 Every AI-generated entry must carry the mandatory SNOMED suffix. Non-compliance breaks downstream audit and safety investigation.
    GV.CR-14 Consultation-Type Appropriateness Assessment (new in v5.3) 🚪 Documented assessment of which consultation types (safeguarding, mental health, paediatrics, intimate exams, end-of-life) AVT is appropriate for vs requires carve-outs. Caldicott Guardian sign-off.
    GV.CR-12 Board-Level AI Governance Mechanism (new in v5.3) 📡 Named board committee or executive director with AI oversight in formal remit, quarterly minimum cadence, escalation path. CQC well-led inspection point.

    Vendor¶

    TP · ASR / Transcription (Technical Pipeline)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    TP.ASR-12 Hallucination-Under-Noise Rate 🚪 Critical pre-deployment test. Whisper-based systems are documented to hallucinate from silence.
    TP.ASR-13 Numeric Accuracy 🚪 Safety-critical and underspecified by current vendor reporting. Should be a Day Zero acceptance criterion.

    TP · Summarisation / NLP (Technical Pipeline)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    TP.SN-5 Hallucination Rate ⚠️ 🔄 Core safety metric. NAS Day Zero SPI with <2% review and ≥5% pause thresholds. See underspecification warning.
    TP.SN-6 Omission Rate 🔄 Arguably more dangerous than hallucination because omissions are invisible to the reviewer.
    TP.SN-15 Negation Handling Accuracy 🔄 Safety-critical and well-documented LLM failure mode.
    TP.SN-20 Uncertainty Marker Preservation 🔄 Certainty inflation creates false diagnostic confidence in the record.

    TP · Clinical Coding (Technical Pipeline)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    TP.CC-6 Code Hallucination Rate 📡 Zero-tolerance metric. Any non-zero rate indicates architectural failure in generation constraints.

    TP · Downstream Write-back (Technical Pipeline)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    TP.WB-1 Write-back Fidelity 🚪 Highest-priority pre-deployment gate. Per-EPR test before go-live.
    TP.WB-2 Integration Error Rate 📡 Standard integration monitoring. Automated, low-burden.
    TP.WB-3 Field Mapping Accuracy 🚪 Wrong-field placement of safety-critical content bypasses downstream safety mechanisms.
    TP.WB-4 Update vs Append Behaviour 🚪 Overwriting existing safety-critical data is a patient safety event.

    GV · Safety & Governance (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.SG-1 Model Version Tracking 📡 Foundation for all continuous assurance. Without knowing which model version produced which output, no performance change is interpretable.

    GV · Operational (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.OP-5 System Availability / Uptime 📡 Standard SLA monitoring. NAS Day Zero SPI (≥99.5%).

    GV · Privacy & Data Governance (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.PD-1 Audio Retention Compliance 📡 UK GDPR storage limitation. Vendor retention practices must align with DPIA.
    GV.PD-9 Cross-Border Data Transfer Compliance 🚪 Legal compliance requirement assessed at procurement.
    GV.PD-10 Subject Access Request Fulfilment 🚪 Vendor must support SAR fulfilment workflow before go-live.
    GV.PD-11 Right to Erasure Compliance 🚪 Vendor must demonstrate erasure scope before go-live.

    GV · Vendor Transparency & Contractual (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.VT-1 Model Change Notification Compliance 📡 Should be a contractual requirement. The Keyes monitoring framework depends on it.
    GV.VT-5 Incident Disclosure Compliance 📡 Without timely incident disclosure, deployers cannot respond to vendor-side security issues.
    GV.VT-7 Sub-Processor Transparency 📡 UK GDPR Article 28 compliance. Assessed at procurement and monitored for changes.
    GV.VT-2 Telemetry Provision Completeness (promoted v5.3) 📡 Per-inference logging, confidence scores, model version per output, intermediate outputs, demographic performance data. The substrate for FTS Performance & Monitoring Response.
    GV.VT-13 Evidence Pack Freshness (promoted v5.3) 📡 All collateral kept up to date. Vendor's responsibility to keep the Hub current.
    GV.VT-14 Indicative Pricing Transparency (promoted v5.3) 🚪 FTS Step 1.a direct submission requirement. Pricing structure transparency for procurement.
    GV.VT-15 Retirement Notification Compliance (new in v4.1) 📡 Notice period before product retirement, feature withdrawal, integration withdrawal.

    GV · NHS Compliance & Regulatory (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.CR-11 Medical Device Classification Documentation (new in v5.3) 🚪 MHRA SaMD classification (Class I / IIa / IIb / III) documented with justification. FTS Step 1.h direct submission.

    Vendor + Deployer + National Body¶

    ES · Meta-Evaluation (Evaluation Science)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    ES.ME-8 Outcome Evidence Commitment Status (promoted v5.3) 🚪 FTS Step 1.f "Evidence of impact and benefit in the NHS" required submission.

    Vendor + National Body¶

    TP · ASR / Transcription (Technical Pipeline)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    TP.ASR-4 Demographic-Disaggregated WER (promoted v5.3) 📡 WER by accent, language, age, speech characteristics. NAS proposes max 5pp gap. MHRA GMLP-3 + Performance & Monitoring "boundaries and bias".

    Regional (ICB) + National Body¶

    GV · Safety & Governance (System Governance)

    Metric Cadence Why this tier
    GV.SG-9 Safety Performance Indicators with Thresholds (DSCMS) 📡 The governance mechanism that converts metrics into action.
    GV.SG-13 Assurance Debt Accumulation Rate 📡 The honest governance metric, surfaced at regional level.
    GV.SG-11 Adverse Event / Incident Rate (LFPSE) 📡 Established national reporting. Needs dedicated LFPSE category for AI/AVT incidents.
    GV.SG-3 Performance Degradation Detection Latency (promoted v5.3) 📡 Time from drift signal to detection. MHRA post-market surveillance (FTS Step 1.i). Limitation-layer detection sitting at regional / national level.

    Disagree, want to propose a metric, or think a tier should shift? This Tier 1 list is a prototype-for-discussion, not a settled checklist. Open an issue at github.com/danjscho/avt-metrics-taxonomy/issues — the roadmap is shaped by reader pushback as much as by the author's pre-baked plan.
